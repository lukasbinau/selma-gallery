      About the artist

      Selma Frausing Binau

      A 22-year-old painter from Brønshøj, Copenhagen, who starts every picture with colour and lets the rest find its way in.

      Selma grew up in Brønshøj, in the quiet streets on the northwest edge of Copenhagen, where the city gives way to allotment gardens, lakes and big open skies. She has been drawing for as long as she can remember, first at the kitchen table and later in a corner of her room that slowly turned into a studio.

      She works in oil, oil pastel and watercolour, often layering them on the same sheet. Her subjects are close to home: faces of the people around her, the fields she passes on summer trips through the Danish countryside, and the ordinary objects on a table when the light falls just right.

      The 2025 & 2026 Collection is her first online exhibition. Walk the room, read the plaques, and if a painting stays with you, it can go home with you.

      Prices and payment

      All prices are in Danish kroner (DKK) and include VAT where applicable. You pay by MobilePay or bank transfer once your order has been confirmed. Each painting is an original, one of a kind, and is reserved for you when the order is placed.

      Delivery

      Pick-up from the studio in Brønshøj is free, by appointment. Delivery within Denmark costs 95 kr. The painting is wrapped, insured and normally arrives within 3–7 working days after payment. We currently deliver to Denmark only.

      Right of withdrawal

      You have a 14-day right of withdrawal, counted from the day you receive the painting. To use it, send a clear message by email before the deadline. Return the painting within 14 days after that, in the condition you received it; you pay for the return shipping. We refund the full purchase price, including the original delivery cost, within 14 days of receiving the painting back.

      Complaints

      You have a 2-year right to complain (reklamationsret) under the Danish Sale of Goods Act. Please contact us first. If we cannot find a solution together, you can complain to Nævnenes Hus / Center for Klageløsning via naevneneshus.dk.

      Privacy

      Selma Frausing Binau is the data controller. When you order, we use your name, email and address only to handle and deliver the order, and share your address only with the shipping carrier. Order records are kept for 5 years, as required by the Danish Bookkeeping Act. You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, and to complain to Datatilsynet (datatilsynet.dk).
