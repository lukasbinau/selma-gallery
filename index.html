      About the artist

      Selma Frausing Binau

      Selma paints faces, fields and the quiet corners of home in bold, saturated colour: cobalt lines, vermilion grounds and yellows that almost hum. She moves freely between oil, oil pastel and watercolour, often layering them on the same sheet.

      This room is her first online exhibition. Walk from painting to painting, read the plaques, and if a piece stays with you, it can go home with you.
